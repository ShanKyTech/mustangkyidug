A journey to the Last Forbidden Kingdom

Stunning high-desert landscapes, ancient monasteries, and a culture untouched by time — Mustang rewards every traveler who makes the journey.

Key attractions

Places that stay with you

Capital of Lo

Lo Manthang

The fortified capital of Upper Mustang — ancient monasteries like Thubchen with breathtaking murals, traditional mud-brick houses, and the historic King’s Palace of Mustang’s last royal line.

3,710 m sacred site

Muktinath Temple

One of the most sacred pilgrimage sites for Hindus and Buddhists alike — its 108 water spouts are believed to wash away sins, while stupas reflect the harmony of two faiths.

Gateway village

Kagbeni

A picturesque village of narrow alleys and green fields at the gateway to Upper Mustang — home to the ancient Kag Chode monastery and the start of legendary trekking routes.

Trekking

Walk the old salt roads

Mustang’s trekking routes attract adventurers and nature lovers from around the world.

Restricted region

Upper Mustang Trek

A journey through a region once closed to outsiders — remote villages like Tangye and Chhusang where time stands still, meditation caves carved into cliffs, and monasteries rich in history and art.

World classic

Annapurna Circuit

One of Nepal’s most famous routes passes through Mustang — from lush valleys to high-altitude desert, with spectacular views of the Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges along the Kali Gandaki gorge.

Cultural experiences

Travel like a guest, not a tourist

Time your visit for a festival

Arrive during Losar or Tiji to witness masked dances, processions, and celebrations that have filled Mustang’s courtyards for centuries.

Taste the local cuisine

Legendary Thakali dal bhat, hand-folded momos, and butter tea — best enjoyed in family-run guesthouses along the trail.

Stay in a homestay

Live with a local family, share their table, and gain a deeper insight into daily life, traditions, and Mustangi hospitality.
