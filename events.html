Festivals & events

Vibrant expressions of our heritage and faith — celebrated in Mustang for centuries, and now in America too.

Signature celebrations

The festivals we keep

Mustang Kyidug USA organizes and promotes these events every year, keeping the spirit of Mustang alive and thriving among those living abroad.

01Festival

Losar — the Tibetan New Year

Feb–Mar≈ 2 weeksCommunity-wide

The most significant festival of the year — a time of renewal, joy, and family reunion. Homes are cleaned and hung with new prayer flags, guthuk soup is shared, monasteries fill with offerings and prayer, and the community gathers for traditional dance and music.

02Festival

Tiji — the chasing of the demons

Spring3 daysLo Manthang

Monks in elaborate masks reenact the story of Dorje Jono subduing the demon that threatened Mustang — three days of theatrical dance, offerings, and prayers for the well-being of the community, celebrated in the walled city of Lo Manthang.

03Gathering

Chhongu — our community gathering

AnnualNew YorkAll ages

The Kyidug’s flagship reunion — traditional performances, workshops that teach the young generation Mustang’s history and language, and a full day of reconnecting with old friends and making new ones.

04Tradition

Dhazang & archery tournaments

AnnualCommunity sport

Mustang’s beloved archery tradition lives on in the diaspora — friendly tournaments and Dhazang gatherings that mix sport, song, and celebration, just as they have in the villages of Mustang for generations.

05Spiritual

Religious events & teachings

Year-roundLed by visiting monks

Prayers, offerings, and meditation sessions led by monks and spiritual leaders from Mustang — plus teachings on Buddhist philosophy, compassion, and mindfulness in daily life.

“A festival is how a community remembers itself — every dance step and every shared meal says: we are still here.” Festivals of Mustang
