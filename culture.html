A living tapestry of tradition

Centuries of history, faith, and craft — woven into the everyday life of the Mustangi people, at home and abroad.

The heart of our culture

Tibetan Buddhism

At the core of Mustang’s culture lies Tibetan Buddhism, which has profoundly shaped the lives and beliefs of its people. The region is home to numerous monasteries — each a spiritual center and a hub of community life.

  • Monasteries as cultural centers — houses like Kag Chode Thupten Samphel Ling offer teachings, rituals, and support; they are places of worship and of learning alike.
  • Living ritual — daily prayers, mask dances, butter lamps, and mantras carried on the wind by prayer flags.
  • Faith that travels — in the USA, monks and spiritual leaders from Mustang lead teachings and ceremonies for the community.

Traditions

What we keep alive

Language

The Lowa language & oral traditions

The Lowa language, a Tibetan dialect, carries our stories, songs, and scriptures. Elders pass down legends of local heroes and deities to younger generations — each story a lesson in values and identity. Preserving Lowa is central to preserving Mustang itself.

Sacred art

Thangka painting & monastery art

Thangka paintings depict Buddhist deities, mandalas, and sacred events in intricate detail. More than beautiful objects, they are teaching tools — windows into the spiritual world that adorn monasteries and homes alike.

Dress

Traditional clothing

The chuba — a warm, ankle-length woolen robe — is worn by men and women; married women wear the colourful pangden apron. Turquoise and coral jewelry, believed to bring fortune and protection, completes the Mustangi dress.

Cuisine

Cuisine of the high desert

Thukpa noodle soup for cold mountain evenings, hearty daal bhat with vegetable curries, and gundruk — fermented greens that carry the taste of home. Mustangi tables are famous for warmth and abundance.

Celebration

Festivals rooted in faith

Vibrant celebrations bring the community together, in Mustang and in America.

Losar

The Tibetan New Year — prayer ceremonies, feasting, and traditional dances to welcome renewal and drive away the old year’s misfortune.

Tiji Festival

Three days of colourful mask dances symbolizing the victory of good over evil — Mustang’s most spectacular monastery festival.

Buddha Jayanti

Honouring the birth, enlightenment, and passing of Gautama Buddha with prayers and offerings at monasteries.
