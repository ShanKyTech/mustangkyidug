The people of Mustang

Resilient, welcoming, and bound by tradition — a unique Himalayan community, at home in the mountains and in America.

Community overview

Shaped by mountains, held together by each other

Historically isolated by rugged mountains and high altitude, the people of Mustang developed a unique identity — Tibetan Buddhist traditions, vibrant festivals, and communal values that place family and village above all. Those same values now hold our community together across the United States.

Ethnic heritage

Two peoples, one Mustang

Mustang is home to two principal communities, each contributing to the region’s cultural richness.

Upper Mustang

The Loba community

The indigenous people of Upper Mustang. Loba culture is deeply Tibetan — Tibetan Buddhism shapes daily life, rituals, and the great community gatherings of the walled city of Lo Manthang and its surrounding villages.

Lower Mustang

The Thakali community

Residing mainly in Lower Mustang along the Kali Gandaki, the Thakali are known for their entrepreneurial spirit — trade, tourism, and a celebrated culinary tradition that has carried Mustang’s name across Nepal and beyond.

In the USA

How the Kyidug serves our people

A support network

Cultural workshops, networking opportunities, and hands-on guidance for newcomers adjusting to life in the USA — no one starts alone here.

Cultural gatherings

Losar and Chhongu celebrations where community members perform, feast, and strengthen the bonds that distance would otherwise loosen.

Empowering youth

Scholarships, mentorship programs, and cultural camps that immerse the next generation in the language, arts, and history of Mustang.

One community

A bond that crosses oceans

The people of Mustang — whether in Nepal or abroad — share a profound connection to their heritage. Mustang Kyidug USA keeps that bond strong: providing support, fostering cultural pride, and empowering future generations.

Through unity and collaboration, the Mustangi community continues to thrive — preserving its traditions while contributing to society at large.
